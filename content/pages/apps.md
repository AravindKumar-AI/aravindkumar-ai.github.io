# apps

small apps for learning, a little at a time.

## [StanZa](stanza.html)

*Language learning · Android*

Indian languages and English, a little practice at a time. Bite-sized learning
cards, personalized quizzes, and spoken roleplays using the learner's own
Sarvam AI key. [Explore StanZa](stanza.html).

## [Mazhalai Suvadi](suvadi.html)

*Preschool learning · Tamil & English · Android*

Suvadi helps little learners aged 2–6 explore Tamil and English through picture
cards, spoken words, alphabets, numbers, and quizzes. Eighteen topics, with
offline learning after downloads. [Explore Suvadi](suvadi.html) ·
[தமிழில்](suvadi-ta.html).
