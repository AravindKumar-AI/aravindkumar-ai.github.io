Three ways to keep learning

Read it. Remember it. Speak it.

Pick the language you want to learn and the topics you enjoy. StanZa gives you a few different ways to make the words your own.

01

One useful card at a time.

Swipe through vocabulary, idioms, phrases, grammar and cultural tips. Hear pronunciation, read examples and rate each card to guide spaced repetition.

02

Practice built around you.

Try multiple-choice, fill-in-the-blank and true-or-false quizzes. AI-generated questions draw on your interests, recent cards and concepts you missed.

03

A conversation worth having.

Practice everyday roleplays with an AI tutor, or try a spoken essay. Get grammar corrections, vocabulary tips and spoken replies.

Make it your practice

A few steps.
A fresh start.

  1. Choose your languages.Tell StanZa your native language and the one you want to learn.
  2. Pick your interests and daily goal.Make practice fit the things you enjoy and the time you have.
  3. Add your Sarvam AI key.Use your key for new AI cards, quizzes and speech. You can skip this during setup and add it later in Profile.

Your key. Your AI practice.

AI content generation, speech recognition and pronunciation require your own Sarvam AI API key and an internet connection. Usage may incur charges under your Sarvam plan.

Your profile and learning archives are saved locally. Text and voice data needed for AI features are sent to Sarvam AI for processing.

AI-generated content and coaching can make mistakes. Treat them as practice guidance alongside other learning resources.

Before you begin

A few questions.

Do I need a StanZa account?

No StanZa account is required. Your profile and learning archives are stored on your device.

Can I use StanZa without a Sarvam AI key?

You can skip the key during setup and add it later in Profile. Previously cached cards remain available; new AI content and audio require a key and internet access.

What can I save?

Bookmark learning cards, conversations and quiz questions so you can return to useful content later.

How do I follow my progress?

Set a daily goal, track practice time and streaks, and follow your in-app language mastery score. The score is a practice indicator, not a proficiency certification.
